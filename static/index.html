BoardCam

Record the game, not the moves

One phone runs the clock. A few times a game, pause it and photograph the board from wherever you sit. Each photo is read into a position you can fix by tapping squares; the moves between photos are filled in afterwards.

Opens the game in progress on this phone, or sets up a new one.
